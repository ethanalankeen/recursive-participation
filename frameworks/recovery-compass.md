# The Recovery Compass

**Definition:** Recovery is revealed by four conditions. The quality of these conditions determines the quality of recovery. The quality of recovery determines available capacity. Capacity determines participation.

**The Formula:** Capacity = Clarity (Focus) + Boundaries (Protect) + Rest (Restore) + Standards (Direct).

**The 4 Conditions:**
- **Clarity:** Focuses energy. Reduces noise. Sharpens attention. Aligns action.
- **Boundaries:** Protects energy. Prevents leakage. Protects time & attention. Builds safety.
- **Rest:** Restores energy. Repairs the body. Integrates the mind. Regulates emotions.
- **Standards:** Directs energy. Sets the bar. Drives decisions. Shapes behavior.

**Key Insight:** "Capacity is not created through effort alone."

**Function:** Calculates and expands available capacity before adding load. Replaces effort-based capacity building.
