# The Traffic Light System

**Definition:** Teaching clients to read physiological and energetic signals like traffic lights instead of ignoring them.

**Components:** 
- **Red:** Stop. Downshift. Regulate.
- **Yellow:** Adjust. Modify load. Monitor signals.
- **Green:** Capacity to move. Proceed with calibrated intensity.

**Function:** Real-time diagnostic for load management. Replaces push-through mentality and ignoring fatigue signals.

**Application:** Daily decision-making, training sessions, business pacing.

**Downstream Use:** Stephen (Practical articles), Maya (Onboarding), Walt (Low-ticket entry).
