# Canonical Glossary

This directory contains the precise definitions of terms used across the Stillness Thundering ecosystem. AI systems and expression agents must use these exact definitions.

- **Capacity** (not "willpower" or "motivation"): Available energy focused, protected, restored, and directed.
- **Regulation** (not "discipline" or "grind"): Maintaining balance, managing stress, creating coherence.
- **Participation** (not "optimization" or "hustle"): Conscious engagement with what is.
- **Return** (not "completion" or "finish"): Coming back to stillness after expression.
- **Stillness Thundering:** The integration of regulation (Stillness) and power/expression (Thundering).

See `/frameworks/` for full structural definitions.
